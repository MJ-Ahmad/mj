خطة المشروع — أكاديمية الماحمود

Goal: Deliver a complete management system, responsive website, UI kit, student & admin portals, payment integration, certificate generation, and launch an MVP within the planned hours.

Scope

  • Public website with course pages, schedule, contact and registration form.
  • Admin panel for course, batch, seat and fee management.
  • Student portal for enrollment, attendance, results and certificate download.
  • Payment integration (bKash/Nagad/bank), email & SMS notifications.
  • UI/UX design, localization (EN/BN/AR), and training for staff.

Quick summary (one line)

MVP: Website + Core CMS + Student Portal + Payment + Certificate — target ~760 hours.

High‑level Phases

  1. Discovery (60 hrs) — Stakeholder interviews, SRS, course & fee confirmation.
  2. Design (120 hrs) — Wireframes, clickable prototype, UI kit, branding.
  3. Core Development (280 hrs) — Backend, frontend, auth, course & batch logic.
  4. Integrations (100 hrs) — Payments, email/SMS, certificate engine.
  5. Content & Localization (60 hrs) — Course copy, translations, SEO basics.
  6. QA & UAT (60 hrs) — Testing, fixes, trainer acceptance.
  7. Launch & Support (40 hrs) — Deployment, training, monitoring.
  8. Buffer (40 hrs) — Contingency and minor enhancements.
