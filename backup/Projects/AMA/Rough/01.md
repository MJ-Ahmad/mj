# 🎯 Project Goal
To design and implement a **comprehensive digital management system** for **Al Mahmud Academy** that includes:
- A modern, responsive **website** showcasing courses, fees, schedules, and contact info.
- A **management system** for student enrollment, fee tracking, scheduling, and certificate issuance.
- A clean, intuitive **UI/UX design** for both administrators and students.
- Integration of **multi-language support** (Bangla, English, Arabic).
- Scalability for future online training modules.

---

# 🗂 Project Scope
- **Website Development**: Responsive site with course details, fees, schedules, and contact forms.
- **Management System**: Student registration, fee management, attendance, certificate generation.
- **UI/UX Design**: Modern, user-friendly interface for web and admin dashboard.
- **Digital Marketing Setup**: SEO, social media integration, Google Maps listing.
- **Future Expansion**: E-learning modules, video classes, online payments.
